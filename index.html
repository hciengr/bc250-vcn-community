Contribution scope

Check task ownership and existing results before starting. Contributions should reproduce a documented check, supply a missing artifact or correct a scoped claim. Preserve original authors and source revisions.

Worker submissions require pinned inputs, exact procedures and raw outputs. Missing inputs and negative results must be recorded. Hardware tests require board-owner authorization.
